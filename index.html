§2.2Gumbel · return period

Rainfall Frequency Analysis

FREQUENCY · GUMBEL DISTRIBUTION

Frequency analysis of annual maximum daily rainfall. The series is screened for completeness of records; statistical parameters (mean, standard deviation, Yn, Sn) are computed; design rainfall is evaluated for each return period. Output is used to assess peak discharge response to land-use change.

§2.3SRGI · GMRT · MSL

Tidal & Bathymetric Analysis

HARMONIC · SRGI · GMRT

Harmonic analysis of sea-level series, followed by grid densification to obtain the amplitude and phase distribution of tidal constituents. Eleven datums are tied to MSL; deviation from the BIG reference is ±0.004 m. GMRT bathymetry is corrected for tide via H = TMA − MSL − D.
